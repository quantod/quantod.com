About

We build AI that runs business processes.

Quantod puts the repetitive processes of a business on AI, so its people spend their time on the decisions only they can make.

We work one process at a time, on real work, and we build every project on our own platform, Cue‑Queue. Each project makes the platform better at the next one, and what we find ourselves building again and again becomes a product anyone can use.

We believe AI in a business should be visible, shared and owned: the people around it should see what it did, work on the same things beside it, and keep what was built.
