How we work

A call, a pilot, then a process that runs.

We do not sell hours. Each engagement puts one business process on AI, with a scope and a price agreed before we start.

The call

Free · 30 minutes

We ask how the process runs today: what comes in, what is done to it, who decides, where the time goes. You leave knowing whether AI can take it on, and how much of it.

The pilot

Fixed scope · fixed price · a few weeks

We put that one process on AI in a workspace of your own and run it on your real cases. Every pilot is different, so the price is set after the call, in writing, before anything starts.

Run and extend

Monthly, when the pilot proves itself

We keep the process running, improve it as it meets the cases nobody thought of, and take on the next process when you are ready.

What you get from a pilot

  • One process running on AI, on your real work
  • A page for it that shows every item and where it stands
  • The process written down as instructions your team can read
  • Your team set up to work alongside it from their own assistant
  • An honest account of what worked and what did not

What we need from you

  • Someone who knows the process and can answer questions
  • Access to the tools and data the process uses
  • A short check-in each week
  • Real cases to run it on, early

Questions

Which processes are a good fit?

Ones with many similar items, a few clear steps per item, and a person who makes the final call: sourcing and screening, outreach and follow-up, reviewing documents or pages against a standard, collecting and organising information. If the work is one-off or every case is entirely different, it is usually not.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your own workspace, under access rules you control, and is used only for your process. We agree in writing what we may access during the pilot.

Which AI assistants do you work with?

Your team can work from Claude or ChatGPT, connected to your workspace. The agents doing the steps can run on the model that suits the task.

What if the pilot does not work?

Then you will know exactly why, which is worth knowing, and you are not committed to anything further. The pilot is priced on its own.

Who owns what we build?

The workspace, its instructions and its data are yours. The platform underneath, Cue‑Queue, is ours, and you can keep using it with or without us.

How much does it cost?

Every process is different, so we do not publish prices. After the call we propose a fixed price for the pilot, and you decide.
