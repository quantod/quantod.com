The problem

The work that eats the week is rarely one big task

It is the same handful of steps on hundreds of things — candidates, leads, documents, pages, orders — passed between people, tracked in spreadsheets and inboxes. AI can do most of those steps now. A chatbot cannot run the process: it forgets where things are, cannot share the work, and nobody can see what it did.

Work comes inEach item — a candidate, a lead, a page — joins a queue.
AI does the stepsAgents research, check, draft and sort, several at once.
People decideYour team sees what is ready and makes the calls that matter.
Nothing is lostEvery item shows where it is, who touched it and what is next.

What is different

Built for work that many hands touch

AI and people on the same work

Several agents and several people can work through the same queue at once without doing anything twice or overwriting each other.

Everything in the open

Every item shows its stage, who handled it and what they decided. No black box, no "the AI did something".

In the assistants you already use

Your team works from Claude or ChatGPT, and from simple pages we build for each process. No new tool to learn first.

You keep it

Examples

Processes that already run this way

Recruitment

Searching for candidates

Before
A recruiter spends days reading profiles against a job brief.
Now
Agents find and screen candidates against the brief, each judgement citing it, and line up the strongest.
People decide
The recruiter reviews a short list on one page and chooses whom to approach.

Business development

Keeping hundreds of relationships warm

Before
Follow-ups slip because nobody can track several hundred contacts by hand.
Now
Agents notice who is worth contacting and why, and draft a message in your voice.
People decide
You approve, edit or skip each one before anything is sent.
Quality

Reviewing many things against a standard

Before
Every page, policy or product listing needs the same careful check, more than once.
Now
Agents check each one against your standard, note what is wrong, fix it, and come back for a second pass.
People decide
You set the standard and settle the cases the agents flag as unclear.
Research

Collecting data from the web

Before
Someone copies details from website after website into a spreadsheet.
Now
Each page to visit is an item in a queue; agents work through it in parallel and the results land in one table.
People decide
You choose what to collect and what to do with it.
