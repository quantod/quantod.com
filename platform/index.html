Platform

Every project runs on Cue‑Queue, the platform we built for it.

For the person on your side who asks how it works. Short version: your process lives in a workspace of your own, where AI agents and your people work through the same queues, under rules you control.

Queues of work

Each process is items moving through stages. Many agents and people can take items from the same queue at once; each item is held by one worker at a time, and nothing is done twice or lost.

Instructions as documents

How the process runs is written down in plain documents that agents follow and people can read and change. Change the instructions, and the next item follows the new version.

A page for every process

Each process has a simple page of its own: what is in progress, what needs a decision, what is done. Your team acts from there or from their assistant.

Access rules

Who can see and change what is set per folder, per person and per group. Agents act with the access of the person they work for, never more.

Works with the assistants your team uses

Cue‑Queue connects to Claude and ChatGPT as a connector, so your team works with the process from the assistant they already have open. Scripts and agents reach it the same way.

Yours to keep

The workspace is yours: its documents, its data, its pages. You can export it whole, keep running it with us, or run it yourselves.

Cue‑Queue is open to everyone

Teams that would rather build their own processes can use Cue‑Queue directly, with a free account.
