Privacy

What this website collects

Very little, on purpose. This website is run by Quantod Pty Ltd (“we”).

On these pages

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. Our hosting provider keeps ordinary server logs (addresses and pages requested) for security, for a short time.

When you book a call

Booking goes through Cal.com, which collects your name, email address and anything you write in the booking form, and sends the invitation. Their privacy policy applies to what they hold. We use what you tell us only to prepare for and follow up on the call.

When you write to us

We keep your email and our replies to answer you and for our records. We do not sell or share them, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Your rights

Ask us at hello@quantod.com to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it.

This notice covers quantod.com. The Cue‑Queue service has its own privacy policy at cue-queue.com.
