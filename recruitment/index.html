A worked example

A candidate search at a recruitment agency

This runs today at a recruitment agency we work with. Names and details are theirs to keep; the shape is this.

1. The brief

The recruiter writes the role, the must-haves and the nice-to-haves as a short document. Every judgement an agent makes later cites the version of the brief it used, so a changed brief is never silently mixed with an old one.

2. Sourcing and screening

Agents find candidates and screen each one against the brief, several at a time, without two of them working on the same person. Each candidate moves through clear stages — found, screened, ready for review.

3. The recruiter decides

One page shows the search as it runs: who is ready for review and why, with the reasoning beside each. The recruiter chooses, rejects with a reason, or asks for more — while the agents carry on.

4. The approach

For each chosen candidate an agent drafts a first message in the recruiter's voice. The recruiter edits it and approves the send; only what they approved goes out.

Business development, too

The same agency keeps hundreds of LinkedIn relationships with clients and prospects warm the same way: agents work out who is worth contacting and why, draft the message, and the recruiter approves, rewrites or skips each one.

  • Your workflows are written down and yours to keep
